Best Headphones for Music Production Under $100

You do not need expensive studio headphones to get started with music production. A good pair under $100 can provide comfortable monitoring and a useful starting point for recording, editing, and mixing.

What should you look for?

When choosing affordable production headphones, focus on comfort, durability, sound consistency, and the type of recording you plan to do.

Closed-back vs. open-back

Closed-back headphones are useful when recording because they help reduce sound leaking from the headphones into a microphone.

Open-back headphones allow more interaction with the surrounding environment and can provide a different listening experience, but they are generally less suitable for recording vocals with a microphone.

Features worth comparing

  • Comfort during long recording sessions
  • Closed-back or open-back construction
  • Cable length and connector type
  • Build quality
  • Replaceable parts when available
  • Compatibility with your recording equipment

Do you need an expensive pair?

Not necessarily. If you are beginning to record music, learning production, or creating content at home, a reliable affordable pair can be enough to get started. More expensive equipment makes more sense when it solves a specific problem in your workflow.

Bottom line

The best headphones are not necessarily the most expensive. Look for a comfortable, durable pair that fits your recording setup and the way you actually work.
