Choosing headphones for music production

When choosing studio headphones, prioritize comfort, consistent sound, durability, and whether you need closed-back or open-back construction.

What to compare

  • Comfort during long sessions
  • Closed-back versus open-back design
  • Cable and connector options
  • Build quality and replacement parts
  • Sound that helps you make reliable decisions

Building a simple home recording setup

You can start with a microphone, headphones, an audio interface when needed, and recording software. Add equipment when it solves an actual problem.

Starter checklist

  • Computer or mobile device
  • Microphone appropriate to your use
  • Comfortable headphones
  • Recording software
  • Mic stand and basic accessories

Creator tech worth considering

Useful creator accessories include storage, stands, cables, lighting, keyboards, USB hubs, and other tools that make a workflow easier.
